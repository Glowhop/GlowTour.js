# Code of Conduct

GlowTour.js aims to maintain a constructive, respectful environment for contributors and users.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- be respectful and considerate in technical discussions
- focus feedback on ideas, code, and observable behavior
- assume good faith while still being precise and direct
- accept constructive feedback and corrections
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or deliberately disruptive behavior

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-managed community spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or lock contributions and interactions that violate these expectations. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

To report conduct privately, email the maintainer at dusartarmand@gmail.com. Do not post sensitive details in public issues or discussions.
